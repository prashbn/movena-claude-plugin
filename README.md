# Movena for Claude

Movena is gym management software for gym owners, their teams and their members. This plugin connects Claude to your Movena account, so you can ask about your gym or your own training in plain language.

## What you can ask

**Gym owners, managers and coaches** can ask for totals from the locations they approve:

- "How many leads does each location have, by status?"
- "Show new leads over 60 days, grouped by week and location."
- "How many members need a check-in at each location, and why?"
- "How busy were our sessions last month?"

**Members** can ask about their own training, and choose what they share.

Your Movena role and location access apply to every request. Claude can read; it can't change anything in Movena.

## Get started

1. Install the plugin from the Claude plugin directory. In Claude Code, you can also run:

   ```
   /plugin install movena --marketplace prashbn/movena-claude-plugin
   ```
2. When Claude first uses Movena, it asks you to connect. Movena opens in your browser.
3. Sign in to Movena, choose what to share and which locations to include, then approve.

## Your data

- Operator reports contain totals, date ranges, statuses and location names only. They never include member names, contact details, notes, messages or health data.
- Movena records each connection and each request in your gym's audit log.
- You can see and remove the connection at any time in Movena, under **Connected assistants**.
